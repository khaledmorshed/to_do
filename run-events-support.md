## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. How do I register for an event?
1. Open the **Run Events** app.
2. Search or select the race you wish to participate in.
3. Tap on the event to open the **Event Details** screen.
4. Click the **Register Now** button to complete your registration directly on the organizer's official page.

### 2. How do I save races to my calendar / favorites?
- Tap the **Bookmark / Heart icon** on any event card or on the event details page to add it to your saved list.
- You can view and manage all saved events under the **My Races** or **Calendar** tab.

### 3. The course map or route is not loading properly. What should I do?
- Ensure your device has an active internet connection.
- Check that location permissions are enabled in your device settings (`Settings > Run Events > Location`).
- Make sure you are using the latest version of the app from the App Store.

### 4. I am an event organizer. How can I list my event?
- Please contact us directly at [faridulhassanb@gmail.com](mailto:faridulhassanb@gmail.com) with your event details, official website, dates, route details, and registration link.

### 5. How do I delete my account or data?
- You can request account deletion directly within the app under **Account / Profile Settings > Delete Account**, or by sending an email request to [faridulhassanb@gmail.com](mailto:faridulhassanb@gmail.com) with the subject *"Account Deletion Request"*.

When reporting a bug, please include the following details so we can assist you quickly:
