# Run Events Child Safety Standards

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

Run Events is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all users. We have **zero tolerance** for any content or behavior that promotes, facilitates, or engages in Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

---

## Our Commitments

We are dedicated to maintaining strict safety standards across our platform through the following actions:

* **Prompt Removal:** Immediately taking down any content that violates our child safety policies.
* **Legal Reporting:** Reporting CSAM and related illegal activities to relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement, as required by law.
* **Law Enforcement Cooperation:** Full cooperation with local and international law enforcement agencies in investigations involving child safety.
* **In-App Reporting:** Providing users with clear tools to report inappropriate content, abusive behavior, or child safety concerns directly within the App.
* **Strict Enforcement:** Taking immediate action—including temporary account suspension or permanent removal—against any users who violate these standards.

---

## Legal Compliance

We comply with all applicable child protection laws, Google Play Developer Policies (including Child Safety Standards), and our legal obligations to actively combat child abuse and exploitation.
