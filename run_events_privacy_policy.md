# Privacy Policy for Run Events

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tyro Solutions** collects, uses, and protects information when you use the **Run Events** mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. About the App

**Run Events** helps users:

* Discover running and sports events
* Register for events
* View event details and locations
* Create and manage events (where applicable)
* Stay updated with upcoming events

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the core features of the App.

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When using the App, you may provide:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Profile information
* Event registration information
* Messages or inquiries sent to our support team

---

### 2.2 Location Information

With your permission, we may collect your device location to:

* Show nearby events
* Improve location-based event discovery
* Display event venues on maps

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings.

---

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

* Device model and type
* Operating system
* App usage statistics
* IP address (for security and analytics)
* Crash reports and diagnostic information

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

* Provide and operate the App
* Enable event registration and participation
* Display nearby and relevant events
* Allow organizers to create and manage events
* Improve App performance and user experience
* Maintain security and prevent fraud or misuse
* Respond to customer support requests

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do **not sell your personal information**.

We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

* **Other users:** Information related to public events you create may be visible to other users.
* **Service providers:** Trusted third-party services that help operate the App (such as cloud hosting, analytics, or notification services).
* **Legal requirements:** When required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

* Access and update your profile information
* Request deletion of your account and associated data
* Control location permissions through your device settings
* Manage notification preferences

---

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to:

* Provide the App's services
* Maintain your account
* Comply with legal obligations

You may request deletion of your account at any time.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.
