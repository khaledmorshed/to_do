# Privacy Policy for Run Events

**Last Updated:** July 29, 2026  
**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Faridul Hassan Bafi** ("we", "us", or "our", developer and publisher of Run Events) collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use the **Run Events** mobile application ("App").

By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. About the App

**Run Events** is an athletic and sports event discovery and registration platform designed to help runners, athletes, and fitness enthusiasts:

* Discover running, marathon, and sporting events locally and globally
* Register and secure tickets for events
* View comprehensive event details, race categories, schedules, and interactive venue maps
* Track personal race history and running milestones
* Create, manage, and promote events (for authorized organizers)
* Receive real-time updates, event reminders, and relevant announcements

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the features and services of the App.

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When creating an account, registering for events, or interacting with our services, you may provide:

* **Account Credentials & Contact Data:** Name, phone number (used for authentication, OTP verification, and critical updates), and email address.
* **Profile Information:** Profile photo or avatar, athlete name, age, gender, emergency contact details, and running club or team affiliations.
* **Event Registration Data:** Selected race categories, t-shirt size, bib preferences, emergency contact information, and registration details required by event organizers.
* **Support Inquiries & Communications:** Any messages, feedback, questions, or inquiries sent to our support channels.

### 2.2 Location Information

With your explicit consent via device permissions, we collect and process your device's geographical location to:

* Show nearby running races and events tailored to your location
* Enhance location-based event search and discovery
* Display event routes, start/finish lines, and interactive venue points on maps

> **Note:** Location permission is optional. You can enable, disable, or adjust location access at any time through your device operating system settings. If disabled, you can still search for events by manually specifying cities, regions, or event names.

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you access and interact with the App, our servers and analytics tools may automatically collect certain diagnostic and technical data, including:

* **Device Information:** Device model, brand, manufacturer, hardware specifications, and operating system version.
* **Usage Statistics:** Features utilized, pages and events viewed, interactions, and interaction timestamps.
* **Network & Diagnostic Data:** IP address (for security, fraud prevention, and regional routing), connectivity type, crash reports, and system performance logs.

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business and service purposes:

* **Service Delivery:** Providing, maintaining, and operating the core features of the App.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do **not sell, rent, or trade your personal information** to third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share your information only under the following limited and necessary circumstances:

* **Event Organizers:** When you register for an event, necessary registration information (such as your name, race category, contact phone/email, and emergency details) is shared with that event’s organizer to facilitate event management, bib assignment, timing, and safety.
* **Public Event Listings:** If you are an organizer creating public events, event information (title, date, location, description) is publicly visible to App users.
* **Service Providers:** Trusted third-party vendors and infrastructure partners who provide essential services on our behalf (e.g., cloud hosting, database management, SMS/OTP delivery, push notifications, payment processing, and error monitoring). These providers are contractually obligated to use your data solely for providing services to us and must adhere to strict confidentiality.
* **Legal and Regulatory Compliance:** When required to do so by applicable law, court order, subpoena, government regulation, or in response to valid legal processes.
* **Safety & Rights Protection:** When we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, integrity, or safety of Run Events, its users, or the general public.

## 5. Account and Data Deletion

We believe in user autonomy and provide straightforward mechanisms to control, export, and delete your data at any time in compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, Google Play User Data policies, and global privacy standards.

### 5.1 How to Delete Your Account and Data Directly in the App

You can initiate permanent account deletion at any time directly from within the App:

1. Open the **Run Events** App.
2. Navigate to the **Account** tab.
3. Under **Settings & General**, tap **Delete Account**.
4. Review the deletion policy and consequences, then tap **Send Delete Request**.
5. Enter your registered phone number and verify your identity using the one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone.
6. Confirm the final deletion prompt.

Once confirmed, your account is immediately deactivated and your personal data will be permanently removed.

### 5.2 Alternative Deletion Request (Email)

If you are unable to access the App or have uninstalled it, you can request full deletion of your account and personal data by emailing our privacy team:

* **Email:** [faridulhassanb@gmail.com](mailto:faridulhassanb@gmail.com)
* **Subject:** `Account Deletion Request - Run Events`
* Please include your registered phone number or email address to enable us to locate and verify your account. Deletion requests submitted via email are verified and processed within **30 days**.

### 5.3 What Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of your deletion request, the following information will be permanently erased:

* **Profile & Account Information:** Your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, and athlete information.
* **Event Registrations:** Your registered race records and upcoming event participation entries.
* **Personal Race History:** Completed race records, finishing times, and performance achievements linked to your account.
* **Activity & Usage Data:** Running activity data, bookmarks, saved events, and search history.
* **Preferences & Settings:** Custom settings, notification preferences, and saved filters.

### 5.4 Information That May Be Retained

As permitted or required by applicable laws and regulations, certain limited records may be retained following account deletion:

* **Financial & Transaction Records:** Payment and billing history retained for statutory accounting, tax audits, and financial reporting obligations.
* **Security & Fraud Prevention:** Anonymized logs and security records retained strictly to investigate security incidents, prevent recurring abuse, or maintain system integrity.
* **Legal & Regulatory Obligations:** Any records that must be preserved pursuant to statutory limitation periods, active litigation, regulatory inquiries, or court orders.

*Any retained information will be isolated, access-restricted, and processed strictly in accordance with this Privacy Policy.*

## 7. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, technical, and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information against accidental, unlawful, or unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, or disclosure.

Measures include:
* HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit across public networks
* Secure cloud storage and database encryption at rest
* Token-based authentication and OTP identity verification
* Regular vulnerability assessments and access controls restricted to authorized personnel

While we make every effort to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
