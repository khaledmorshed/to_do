# Privacy Policy for Gas Near Me

**Last Updated:** May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tyro Solutions** collects and uses information when you use the **Gas Near Me** mobile application (“App”).

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. About the App

**Gas Near Me** helps users:
- Add gas shops
- View nearby gas shops based on location
- Discover and share shop information

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide core features of the App.

### 2.1 Information You Provide

When using the App, you may provide:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Location details
- Shop information (if you add a shop), such as:
  - Shop name
  - Address
  - Contact details
  - Other related details

If you contact support, we may also collect your message and contact details.

---

### 2.2 Location Information

With your permission, we may collect your device location to:

- Show nearby gas shops
- Improve search accuracy

You can disable location access anytime from your device settings.

---

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical data such as:

- Device type and model
- Operating system
- App usage data (features used, time spent)
- IP address (for security and analytics)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

- Provide and operate the App
- Show nearby gas shops based on location
- Allow users to add and manage shop listings
- Improve App performance and features
- Ensure security and prevent misuse
- Respond to user support requests

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do **not sell personal data**.

We may share limited data only in these cases:

- **Other users:** Shop details you publicly add will be visible to others
- **Service providers:** Hosting or analytics services (if used)
- **Legal requirements:** If required by law or government request

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

- Access and update your profile information
- Delete your account and data (by request)
- Control location permissions via device settings
- Manage app notifications

---

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your data.  
However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to:

You may request deletion of your account at any time.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover such data, it will be deleted immediately.
